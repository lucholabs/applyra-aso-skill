# Seasonal campaigns and In-App Events

Read for holiday/trend opportunities, temporary listing changes or event briefs. Treat calendar interest as a hypothesis until relevant market evidence supports it. A campaign plan does not authorize a store update, chart note or scheduled automation.

## Market calendar and eligibility

Use local dates, year, timezone, culture, school/holiday calendars and product availability. Verify dates from authoritative local sources when they affect a decision; do not reuse a US calendar worldwide. Choose only occasions the existing product can genuinely serve. A generic discount, routine bug fix or invented capability is not automatically an eligible Apple In-App Event.

Capture the user objective, target market, seasonal use case, evidence, production/review lead time, release dependencies, budget/capacity, owner, start/end and restore date in [the seasonal calendar](../assets/seasonal-calendar.template.csv). No fixed promise of indexing/review turnaround. Plan a buffer using current submission requirements and actual team history; do not label screenshot changes review-free.

## Keyword and listing plan

1. Reuse product context and relevant Applyra research. Discover/inspect terms only within budget and with exact market context. Applyra does not expose a dedicated trending-query feed or historical search-volume series; a current traffic score is not evidence of a seasonal spike. Use comparable saved snapshots or another dated source, otherwise label demand unvalidated.
2. Preserve proven evergreen phrases and use available text budget or weaker relevant terms for experimentation. Compare complete copy packages with [metadata simulation](13-aso-health-simulation.md), including lost phrases and quota limits.
3. Choose the appropriate surface: promotional text, targeted listing, creative, event or main metadata. Record eligibility, localization, approval/publication state and how it reaches the intended audience. Do not force title/keyword changes when a lighter surface suffices.
4. Save the prior values, intended live window and exact rollback values. A planned restore date does not mean the restoration is scheduled. Verify actual live state before recording publication or reverting an unrelated later change.

Measure discoverability with Applyra and conversion/event outcomes with store/product analytics when available. Keep traffic mix, paid campaigns, competitor activity and seasonality as confounders. Stop or revert if the offer expires, the event is unavailable, a claim becomes inaccurate, routing breaks or protected phrases/quality guardrails materially deteriorate.

## Apple In-App Event brief

Use [the event brief template](../assets/event-brief.template.md) when an eligible, time-bound experience actually exists. Verify current rules before generating submission-ready copy. Include:

- Product evidence and event type/badge; distinguish a real event from everyday use or a metadata promotion.
- Target audience, territories/locales, availability/eligibility and user benefit.
- Localized name, short/long descriptions and current field counts/limits with official sources. The bundled listing validator does not validate event-field limits; count and check these separately.
- Event-card/detail media brief with required dimensions, rights, real product proof, accessibility and localized alternatives.
- Start/end/promotion times with timezone, deep link, destination and QA for installed/uninstalled, signed-in/out and region-ineligible users as applicable.
- Submission owner, lead time/dependencies and proposed/submitted/approved/published state, each with evidence when observed.
- Measurement source, primary metric, guardrails and end/rollback behavior. Do not promise featuring or search ranking gains.

Google Play promotional content is a separate surface with its own eligibility and asset requirements; verify those rather than translating an Apple event submission unchanged. If unavailable, propose a relevant listing/creative plan and record the eligibility gap.

## Outputs

For a focused request, return the calendar/brief inline. For saved work use `docs/aso/campaigns/seasonal-calendar.csv` and `docs/aso/campaigns/event-brief.md`, with change logs and surface state only for the planned changes. Suggest follow-up dates; create automation only when requested and supported. Publication remains a separate action requiring authorization and a real store tool.

Sources checked 2026-11-02: [Apple In-App Events](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/in-app-events/), [manage events](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/offer-in-app-events/manage-events), [Google promotional content](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12929944).
