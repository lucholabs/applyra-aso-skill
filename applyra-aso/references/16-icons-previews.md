# Icon audits and preview briefs

Read for an icon audit, creative concepts or app-preview/promo-video brief. Use [creatives and conversion](08-creatives-conversion.md) for intent, store surfaces and experiment design. A brief does not imply that assets were generated, rendered, uploaded or tested.

## Evidence and platform

Inspect the actual current icon/assets and real app screens when available. Record the target store, device/form factor, locale, listing surface, audience, acquisition source and verified promise. Without assets, provide a provisional brief and identify what needs inspection; do not invent a visual teardown. Use current Apple/Google documentation for delivery specifications; one platform's video rules or icon export requirements do not apply to the other.

## Icon audit

Inspect at thumbnail size in a representative search-results composition, not only on a full-size canvas. Compare relevant competitor icons in the same context. Evaluate recognizable shape, text legibility, contrast in the actual store UI, distinctiveness, product/brand fit and cultural interpretation. Record observations with screenshots or asset paths, uncertainty and specific changes; do not turn subjective design scores into a measured conversion forecast.

When alternatives are justified, brief distinct concepts that vary a clear hypothesis, such as symbol simplicity, contrast or feature emphasis. Preserve recognizable brand cues and avoid competitor imitation. Test the proposed mark at small sizes and on relevant backgrounds. Separate the marketing icon from runtime adaptive/masked or alternate-icon assets; verify the target export specification before production.

## Preview storyboard

Use [the creative brief template](../assets/creative-brief.template.md) for the requested icon or preview; omit irrelevant sections. For a video include:

| Segment | Purpose | Required detail |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | Prove the primary benefit quickly | Exact real screen/state, hook text, search intent |
| Demonstration | Show how the app delivers it | User action, screen transitions, product limitations |
| Differentiator | Demonstrate a verified advantage | Visible evidence; no fabricated social proof |
| Resolution | Show the useful outcome | Actual final state and platform-appropriate ending |

Give each beat timecodes, capture source, visual/action, overlay, audio/captions and localization notes. Fit duration to current store requirements rather than making every app use the same pacing. Choose the poster frame deliberately and verify it remains understandable with autoplay off. Design for muted playback; captions and overlays must remain readable on small screens and in longer translations/RTL.

Apple previews demonstrate the real app with footage captured on device. Verify the current delivery specification for count, duration, dimensions, codec and frame rate before rendering. For Google Play, verify the current promo-video/YouTube requirements separately. Never assume lifestyle footage or ad-style claims valid on one store are valid on both.

## Experiment and handoff

State control, concept/treatment, expected mechanism, primary store metric, guardrails, audience, required traffic/evidence and decision rule. Use platform experiment evidence; do not promise uplift or infer a winner from aesthetic preference. Alternate iOS icons for PPO must be present in the live binary; use the existing surface workflow.

Deliver an audit/brief inline for a focused request or save an icon brief / preview storyboard under `docs/aso/creatives/`. Asset generation or video production requires the user's requested scope and an available tool/workflow. Label reference images, concepts and finished assets accurately; store screenshots must remain faithful to available product behavior.
